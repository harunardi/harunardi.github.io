excerpt: 'Neutron noise simulations for 2D rectangular geometries have been done for two cases, homogeneous reactor and C3 benchmark. The results agree with the analytical comparison and code-to-code comparison. For both cases, the ZPRTF shows a bell-shaped curve for the phase, with plateau visible in the magnitude. This behavior is expected in all cases. Overall, the simulation shows the effect of perturbation in a 2D system from a different perspective.'
